# Card catalogue

The seeded catalogue contains **30 cards: 24 Creatures and 6 typeless Power
cards**. This page lists their base stats, moves, and effects. Card details in
the game use server-provided data; battles use the values saved when the match
was created.

| Rarity    | Creatures | Power cards | Total |
| --------- | --------: | ----------: | ----: |
| Common    |        11 |           3 |    14 |
| Rare      |         5 |           1 |     6 |
| Epic      |         5 |           1 |     6 |
| Legendary |         3 |           1 |     4 |

## Getting these cards

Starters contain the 3 Common Creatures of your chosen **Wit, Grit, or
Spark** affinity, plus **Field Kit** and **Reserve Focus**. The random-reward
pool contains the 16 non-evolved Creatures and all 6 Power cards. The 8
evolved Creatures come from their [evolution recipes](../requirements/card-evolution-rules.md);
owned copies can also be obtained through [trading](../requirements/card-trading-policy.md).

The fixed CPU deck uses **Quillip**, **Voltiki**, **Karrik**, **Field Kit**, and
**Reserve Focus**. See [deck rules](../requirements/basic-game-rules.md#saved-decks)
for composition and rarity costs, and
[battle rules](../requirements/battle-rules.md#type-matchups) for affinity matchups.

## Power-card catalogue

Power cards have no affinity or Creature stats. They apply their effects in the
listed order and are discarded after use.

| Card           | Rarity    | Effect                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------- | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Field Kit      | Common    | Heal the active Creature by 30 HP.                                                                                                                                    |
| Reserve Focus  | Common    | Restore 5 Power to the active Creature.                                                                                                                               |
| Heavy Impact   | Common    | Deal 30 neutral damage to the opposing Creature.                                                                                                                      |
| Counterplan    | Rare      | Drain 2 opposing Power, then reduce the opposing Creature's damage by 15% for its next 2 turns.                                                                       |
| Grand Strategy | Epic      | Heal 20 HP, restore 3 Power, then increase the active Creature's damage by 20% for its next 2 turns.                                                                  |
| Fate Reversal  | Legendary | Swap the active Creatures' current HP percentages, applied to each Creature's own maximum HP and rounded down to at least 1 HP. Both players need an active Creature. |

## Effect semantics

- Effects resolve in order. If an effect ends the match, later effects stop.
  Reapplying the same modifier refreshes its duration or charges rather than
  stacking another instance.
- **Poison** deals neutral damage, can defeat a Creature, and remains after its
  source is defeated. Reapplication refreshes its duration to 3 turns.
- **Stun** blocks Creature moves while still allowing a legal Power-card action.
  It does not stack; recovery grants immunity to another stun.
- **Dodge** makes a server-resolved roll on the next opponent-directed damage
  effect from an incoming Creature move. The charge is used whether the roll
  succeeds or fails; ordered self-effects and costs still apply.
- **Reflect** returns a percentage of actual HP damage received, rounded down.
  It does not reduce the incoming damage or lower the attacker below 1 HP.
- **Lifesteal** heals from actual HP damage dealt, rounded down. It does not
  apply to the move that grants it.
