# Basic-tier Decision Register

## Status and authority

This register is the issue #62 consolidation point for the remaining Wits Quest
Basic-tier product and governance decisions. It was prepared on 2026-08-13 by
Kevin Ebrahim, Product and Player Experience Lead, for review by Caleb Pather,
Cards, Game Systems and Quality Lead.

The labels used below are deliberate:

- **Confirmed** — supported by an approved decision record or an unambiguous
  current Gitea issue contract.
- **Blocked** — implementation must wait for the named owner to record the
  missing choice. A proposal elsewhere is not authority to invent the answer.
- **Deferred** — explicitly outside the Basic release.
- **Superseded** — an older statement conflicts with a newer or more
  authoritative decision and must not drive implementation.

The linked Gitea issue or comment is the evidence source. Approval of this
register consolidates those sources; it does not retrospectively invent a
stakeholder decision.

## Blocking decision requests

The following inputs are required before issue #62 can be considered complete:

1. **Caleb Pather — card, deck, and battle rules:** provide rarity points;
   attribute/stat ranges; legal move/effect representation and values; the
   affinity cycle and modifiers; and the complete deterministic battle/tie rule.
2. **Tyron Van Tonder and Uzair Mahomed — account lifecycle:** approve the
   deletion, pseudonymisation, retention, sequencing, and partial-failure policy
   for every data class listed in `BASIC-12`.
3. **Tyron Van Tonder — roles:** define Administrator powers, Author inheritance,
   privileged-role assignment/revocation, and the minimum audit trail.
4. **Kevin Ebrahim — map provider and external-service acceptance:** run the
   Wits-campus comparison described in the map-provider evaluation, record the
   selected provider and cost/licensing owner, and link client, lecturer, or brief
   evidence confirming that the selected integration satisfies the external-service
   requirement.

## Implementation gate

Work may proceed on confirmed, separable foundations, but an implementation must
not guess a blocked value. In particular:

- #63 must not implement destructive account deletion before `BASIC-12` is
  resolved;
- #71 may establish catalogue structure and confirmed vocabulary but must not
  invent stats, move values, or type modifiers;
- #72 may establish ownership and saved-deck structure but cannot enforce the
  ten-point cap until rarity values exist;
- #74 and dependent match issues remain blocked until `BASIC-09` and `BASIC-10`
  are resolved; and
- #66 and #68 must implement `BASIC-03` final-score-only feedback; the conflicting
  per-question, correct-answer, and explanation wording in those issue bodies is
  superseded, and accepted answers remain private.
