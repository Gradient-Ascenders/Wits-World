# Getting started as a player

## Sign in and choose a name

1. Choose **Sign in** in the app navigation. Auth0 opens the sign-in page;
   sign in with your existing account or use its signup option to create one.
2. If prompted, choose your **Player Name** and select **Enter Wits World**.
   This is the name other players use to find you for battles and trades.
3. Open **Explore** to browse campus events. Allow location access when you
   want to check whether you are close enough to start a challenge.

Player Names must be unique and contain **3–20 characters**. Use letters,
numbers, and any of this punctuation:

```text
_ + - . ! # $ ^ ` ~ @ '
```

Spaces, email addresses, and phone-number formats are not allowed. Names are
saved in lowercase and cannot be changed in the current app. If a name is taken
or invalid, correct it and submit again. Email verification is not required for
game access and does not verify a Wits institutional identity.

## Claim your starter cards

Open **Cards** at `/collection`. Choose **Wit**, **Grit**, or **Spark**, inspect
the offered cards, and confirm your choice. You receive three Common Creatures
of that affinity and the Power cards **Field Kit** and **Reserve Focus**.

You can claim a starter only once and cannot change its affinity afterwards.
To use these cards in battle, [create a saved deck and make it active](player-deck-building-journey.md).
Claiming cards does not create or select a deck for you.

## Choose what to do next

- [Explore campus](player-location-journey.md) and [play a challenge](player-challenge-journey.md)
  to earn cards and points.
- [Play a CPU battle](player-cpu-battle-journey.md) with your saved deck.
- [Edit your profile](../design/profile-showcase.md) and choose what others can see.
- [Adjust accessibility settings](../design/design-system.md#accessibility-settings)
  or read the [account settings guide](player-account-settings.md).

If sign-in or account loading fails, use the page's retry control. For a forgotten
password or a different sign-in account, see [Account settings](player-account-settings.md).
