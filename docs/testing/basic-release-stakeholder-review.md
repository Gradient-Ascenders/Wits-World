## Demonstration and decision checklist

- Account registration, sign-in, recovery, profile, logout, deletion boundary,
  and current Auth0 email policy.
- Event discovery, map/list relationship, availability/reachability language,
  one-shot reported location, reward-once behaviour, and current field gaps.
- Multiple-choice and ordering challenge rules, timing, answer locking,
  correctness/explanation feedback, and failure states.
- Reward, collection, thirty-card catalogue, starter grant, owned-copy and
  evolution boundaries relevant to the Basic journey.
- Five-card deck composition, owned-copy rules, rarity cap, selected deck, and
  authoritative validation feedback.
- CPU turn/action rules, status/effect communication, completion, and private
  match history.
- Mobile, keyboard, screen-reader, contrast, reduced-motion, touch, zoom, and
  map-independent expectations.
- Deployed Web/API/database/docs boundary, OpenAPI availability, recovery,
  monitoring, and known limitations.
- Poor-GPS decision: ADR-006 remains **Proposed/deferred pending stakeholder and
  group confirmation**. Issue #167 does not implement or accept QR proof.
